CHOIKIKI

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the iOS app "Choikiki" (ちょい聴き, the "App") handles information about its users. The App is provided by Keisuke Urago (the "Developer").

1. Information the Developer collects

The Developer does not collect any personal information through the App. The App has no user accounts and does not send data to any server operated by the Developer.

2. Information that stays on your device

The following information is stored and used only on your device and is never sent to the Developer.

  • The folder and audio files you choose: They are read only to list and play them. File contents and file names are never sent anywhere.
  • A reference to the last folder you opened, and your settings (such as banner position and display options): These are saved on your device so they can be restored the next time you open the App.

3. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC. To serve ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, Google may collect and use information such as:

  • The advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking
  • Device information such as IP address, device model and OS version
  • Ad interactions such as impressions and taps
  • Approximate location inferred from your IP address

When you first open the App, it asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you do not allow tracking, the advertising identifier is not used and you will see non-personalized ads. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on your iPhone.

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and similar regions, the App asks for your consent to advertising through Google's consent management tool. You can change your choice from "Ad privacy settings" (広告のプライバシー設定) in the App's settings screen.

For details on how Google handles information, please see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

4. In-app purchases

The in-app purchase that removes ads is processed by Apple through the App Store. Payment information is handled by Apple, and the Developer never receives your credit card number or other payment details. The App only checks, on your device, the purchase records provided by Apple to determine whether you have made the purchase.

  • Apple Privacy Policy

5. Sharing with third parties

Apart from the advertising and payment services described above, the Developer does not share your information with any third party.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13, and the Developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when necessary. Any changes take effect when they are posted on this page.
