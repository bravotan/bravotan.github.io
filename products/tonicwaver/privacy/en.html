TONICWAVER

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the iPad app "Tonicwaver" (the "App") handles information about its users. The App is provided by Keisuke Urago (the "Developer").

1. Information the Developer collects

The Developer does not collect any personal information through the App. The App has no user accounts and does not send data to any server operated by the Developer. It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking.

2. Information that stays on your device

The following information is stored and used only on your device and is never sent to the Developer. It is removed when you delete the App.

  • Your settings (such as key, scale, volume and display options): saved on your device so they can be restored the next time you open the App.
  • Favorites: the keys, scales, hand shapes and other settings you save are kept on your device.
  • The number of free uses of the circle of fifths: kept on your device to count uses per hour.

3. Your performance audio

"Replay" temporarily keeps the last few minutes of the App's own audio output in your device's memory. The microphone is never used, and no surrounding sound or voice is recorded. The audio becomes a file only when you export it, and you choose where that file goes through the share sheet.

4. In-app purchases

The in-app purchase (the circle of fifths add-on) is processed by Apple through the App Store. Payment information is handled by Apple, and the Developer never receives your credit card number or other payment details. The App only checks, on your device, the purchase records provided by Apple to determine whether you have made the purchase.

  • Apple Privacy Policy

5. Sharing with third parties

Apart from the payment service described above, the Developer does not share your information with any third party.

6. Children

Because the App does not collect personal information, it does not collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when necessary. Any changes take effect when they are posted on this page.
